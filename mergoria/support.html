How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On the old device, sign in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account; your progress is saved to your account. Sign in with the same account on the new device and your progress arrives.

The "Which progress should stay?" screen appeared

Your account has a different progress saved from another device. Level, gold, gems and last played time of both sides are shown side by side; the side you do not pick is not deleted, it is backed up on your device and can be restored from Settings → Account → Backups.

My purchased gems did not arrive

If the purchase is awaiting approval (e.g. Family Sharing approval), it is added once approved. If the connection dropped, the store delivers the purchase again when you reopen the game and the content is added once. If the problem persists, write to us with the order number from your receipt.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Sign in with Apple or Google on your old device, then sign in with the same account on the new one; your progress syncs.

Turning music or sounds off

Music, Sound effects and Haptics can each be turned on or off in Settings.
