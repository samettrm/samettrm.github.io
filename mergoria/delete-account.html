Account and data deletion

Türkçe

Below is how to delete your account and data in the MERGORIA game (BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.). An account is optional; sign-in uses your Apple or Google account.

Deleting in the game (recommended)

  1. Open the game and tap the gear icon (Settings) on the resource panel at the top left.
  2. Tap the Account row.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm by holding the button.

Deletion request by email

If you have removed the game or played without signing in, write to bsdigitalapp@gmail.com stating the Apple or Google account you signed in with. We complete the request within 30 days after verifying your identity.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account, your sessions and your cloud save on the server
  • If you used Sign in with Apple, the link at Apple: the stored token is revoked with Apple
  • Usage data is unlinked from your account; it is deleted automatically after at most 13 months

Data that remains

  • The save on this device may remain on the device; it is removed when you delete the game.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple and Google under their rules. Deleting the account does not refund purchases.
