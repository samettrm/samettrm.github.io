MERGORIA

Merge and restore an Aegean town

Türkçe

MERGORIA is a merge game in which you bring an Aegean seaside town back to life. Merge coffee beans, tools and dough on the board, deliver the townsfolk's orders, and restore the café, the bakery, the fish stall and the pier stage by stage. Earn Town Stars in the Market Puzzle and befriend the people of the town. The game is played in landscape and needs no account; if you wish, sign in with Apple or Google to carry your progress across your devices.
