In short

MERGORIA is played without an account; your progress is kept on your device. If you sign in with Apple or Google, your progress is stored on our server with your account so it can sync across your devices. To improve the game we collect product usage data tied to a random installation ID; your IP address is not stored. The only ads in the game are Google AdMob rewarded ads that play when you choose. Purchases are processed by Apple and Google; your card details never reach us. Your data is not sold.

1. Account (optional)

The whole game can be played without an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google:

  • The identity token from Apple/Google is verified on our server and not stored. Your account is matched to the account identifier the provider gives you. Your name is not sent to the server; your email address is not stored.
  • For your session the server issues a short-lived access token and a refresh token; the refresh token is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • With Sign in with Apple, the refresh token Apple provides is stored encrypted so that we can remove the link at Apple when you delete your account; it is revoked with Apple when the account is deleted.

2. Game progress and cloud save

Your save (board, resources, town stages, puzzle, friendships, settings and the IDs of purchase transactions whose content has been granted) is kept on your device. If you are signed in, it is also stored on our server with your account to sync across your devices; only you can read it. Up to three backups are kept on your device before the save is replaced.

3. Product usage data

To understand how the game is used and to find bugs, the following events tied to a randomly generated installation ID are sent to our server: first open, session, tutorial start and completion, completion of a building stage (building and stage number), start, win or loss of a puzzle level (level number), the store rating prompt being requested (your rating never reaches us), and a purchase starting or completing. Each event records the platform (iOS/Android), app version and build number, distribution channel, simulator flag and the country the connection came from (two-letter code; the IP address is not stored). If you are signed in, events are linked to your account. Name, email, location and advertising ID are never added to these events.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The only ads in the game are Google AdMob rewarded ads: an ad plays only when you tap the button, and you earn +20 energy or a puzzle booster in return. There are no banners, interstitials or app-open ads; the game never opens an ad on its own.

To show and measure ads and prevent abuse, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends the following from your device directly to Google: IP address (to estimate general location), ad interactions (impressions, taps, video views), diagnostics and device identifiers (on Android the advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone and iPad the advertising ID only if you allow it). This data does not reach our server; Google processes it under its own policies: Google advertising · Google Privacy Policy.

Permission. On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking permission prompt appears; if you do not allow it, only non-personalized ads are requested and the game works the same. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads are requested. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Purchases

Gem packs and the starter pack are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play; the store collects the payment and your card details never reach us. A pack's content is granted after the store has verified the purchase; the transaction ID is kept in your save (in the cloud save if you are signed in) so the same purchase is never granted twice.

Update notifications

To send you a single notification when a new version of the game is released, your device's notification token, language preference and installation ID are stored on our server. The notification is delivered through Expo via Apple's notification service on iPhone and Google Firebase on Android. If you have not allowed notifications, no token is collected. No promotional or marketing notifications are sent through this channel; the token is deleted if unused for 13 months.

6. Transfers and infrastructure

Our server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1); sign-in is provided by Apple and Google; ads are served by Google AdMob; purchases are processed by Apple and Google. As these providers may be located abroad, processing may involve international transfers of personal data. Your data is not sold, and data on our server is not shared for advertising or marketing.

7. Retention

Your account and cloud save are kept until you delete the account. Product usage data and the installation record are kept for at most 13 months and then deleted automatically; ended session records are deleted after 30 days. When you delete your account, usage data is unlinked from it.

8. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect account data from children under 13.

9. Your rights (KVKK art. 11 / GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to processing and data portability. You can delete your account in the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. Details: Account and data deletion. For other requests: bsdigitalapp@gmail.com.
