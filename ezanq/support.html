Why do the times differ from the official calendar by a few minutes?

When official Diyanet data is not connected, times are calculated astronomically on the device. With the Diyanet method, 2 minutes are added to Asr, Maghrib and Isha as a precaution so that iftar never shows before the official time. You can see which source is used on Settings → Where do the times come from?.

Notifications do not arrive

  • Check that Settings → Prayer notifications is on and that EzanQ has notification permission in system settings.
  • On Android, battery saving can delay notifications: remove battery restrictions for EzanQ in system settings.
  • Opening the app now and then is enough; notifications are rescheduled on the device for the coming days.

The Qibla compass looks wrong

Hold the phone flat and move away from metal, magnets or electronics. If the compass asks for calibration, move the phone in a figure "8". The Qibla bearing is written on screen even without a compass.

How do I move Pro to a new phone?

Tap Restore on the Pro screen with the same Apple ID or Google account. If you signed in with Apple or Google in the app, Pro returns automatically when you sign in with the same account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

App Store: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Google Play: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. Details: Account and data deletion.
