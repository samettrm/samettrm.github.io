2. The service

EzanQ shows prayer times, the time left until the next prayer, the Qibla direction and the Ramadan timetable, and sends prayer notifications. Times are calculated on your device from the coordinates of the location you choose and the calculation method you select; when official Diyanet data is connected, that data is shown. The app always states which source is used on the Where do the times come from? screen.

3. Nature of the times

Times calculated on the device can differ from the official calendar by a few minutes; with the Diyanet method, 2 minutes are added to Asr, Maghrib and Isha as a precaution. The Qibla compass depends on your device's magnetic sensor and can drift near metal or electronics. The official calendar of your location prevails.
