Account and data deletion

Türkçe

This page explains how to delete your account and data in EzanQ (BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.). An account is optional; sign-in uses your Apple or Google account.

Delete in the app (recommended)

1. Open the app and tap the Settings tab.
2. Tap the Account row.
3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you are not signed in, Delete usage data for this device on the same screen deletes this installation's records.

Request by email

If you have removed the app, write to bsdigitalapp@gmail.com stating the Apple or Google account you signed in with. We verify your identity and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and synced settings (saved cities, preferences)
  • All usage records linked to your account and this device's installation record
  • If you signed in with Apple, the connection with Apple: the stored token is revoked with Apple

What is kept

  • Store transaction records (purchase date, product, amount) are kept for the legally required period as financial records.
  • Settings on this device stay on the device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it separately in your store account.

EzanQ is an independent app and is not the official app of the Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet). “Diyanet method” names the calculation method.
