Effective: 23.10.2026 · Data controller: BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.

In short

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your device. Your location is used for that calculation and is not sent to our server. An account is optional: prayer times, Qibla, notifications and the Ramadan timetable work without one. The app shows no ads, and your data is not sold.

1. Location

Location permission is requested only when you tap Use my location, and location is used only while the app is open (never in the background). Coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla. To turn the location into a place name, your device's operating system (Apple on iOS, Google on Android) uses its geocoding service. You can also pick a city from the list; then location permission is never requested.

2. Notifications

Prayer notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to our server for notifications and no remote (push) notifications are used.

3. Account (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or Google. Signing in syncs your saved cities and preferences across your devices and links Pro to your account.

  • The identity token from Apple/Google is verified on our server and not stored. Your account is kept as a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your provider identifier; your name and email are not stored on our server and are only shown on your device.
  • Synced settings are stored on our server: your saved cities (name, country, approximate coordinates and time zone), selected city, calculation method, notification preferences, theme and language. Only you can read this document.
  • With Sign in with Apple, the refresh token Apple issues is stored encrypted so that we can revoke it with Apple when you delete your account.

4. Product usage data

To improve the app, the following is sent to our server:

  • a randomly generated installation ID (a new one is created when the app is reinstalled; it is not your name, email or a device identifier),
  • the name and time of product events: first open, app open, onboarding started and completed, sign-in, Qibla opened, notifications enabled, Pro screen opened, plan selected, purchase started/completed/cancelled/failed, restore; small details tied to the event (plan type, where the screen was opened from),
  • platform (iOS/Android), OS and app version, build number, device type (phone/tablet), whether it is a simulator, and your device's region and language settings,
  • the country the connection comes from (two-letter code; the IP address is not stored),
  • if you are signed in, your account hash (the SHA-256 hash above).

Location, saved cities, name and email are never added to these events.

5. Purchases

Pro purchases are processed by Apple and Google; your card details never reach the app. Entitlement is verified through RevenueCat, which processes the purchaser ID (random when not signed in, your account ID when signed in), store, product, price and country. Store notifications are recorded on our server with transaction ID, product, store, country, amount and environment (live/test).

6. Official prayer time data

When data from the Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet) is connected, the app requests the selected city's monthly times from our server using only the city number. Neither your location nor your identity is sent.

7. Infrastructure and transfers

Our server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1); purchase verification uses RevenueCat; sign-in uses Apple and Google services. These providers may be located outside Türkiye, so processing may involve transfers abroad. Data is not sold and not shared for advertising or marketing.

8. Retention

Your account and synced settings are kept until you delete the account. Usage records are kept until you delete them; no automatic deletion is applied on the server today. Store transaction records are kept for the legally required period as financial records.

9. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect account data from children under 13.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to processing and to data portability. You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. If you are not signed in, Settings → Account → Delete usage data for this device deletes this installation's records. Details: Account and data deletion. For other requests: bsdigitalapp@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date is updated and the current version is published at this address.
