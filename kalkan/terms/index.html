1. What KALKAN does

KALKAN filters unwanted SMS/MMS messages and screens unwanted calls, entirely on your device. On Android it operates as your default SMS app; see §4.

2. What KALKAN does not promise

Spam filtering is a judgement, not a certainty. KALKAN does not guarantee that every unwanted message will be caught, or that no legitimate message will ever be held back. You remain responsible for reviewing quarantined items.

We make no claim of complete protection, and we do not publish an accuracy figure as a promise of real-world performance.

3. KALKAN Pro subscription

Downloading KALKAN is free. Spam and scam filtering, call protection and the Check tools work with a KALKAN Pro subscription; without it these features are off. KALKAN Pro includes all of the following: spam and scam filtering, call protection, the Check tools, quarantine, allow/block lists, advanced rules, number range and prefix blocking, sensitivity control and local statistics.

  • Length and price: KALKAN Pro is a yearly, auto-renewing subscription. The price is shown by the store in your local currency before you buy. There is no free trial.
  • Payment: Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Automatic renewal: The subscription renews automatically for the same length at the then-current price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Managing and cancelling: On iPhone, Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; KALKAN Pro keeps working until then.
  • Restoring: On devices signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account, use Restore in the app to use your subscription.
  • Earlier purchases on Android: If you bought KALKAN on Google Play when it was a paid app, KALKAN Pro is unlocked for you for life; you will not be asked to pay again.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

The app shows no ads. For the version downloaded from the App Store, Apple's Standard License Agreement (EULA) applies together with these terms.

4. Android default SMS role

To quarantine a message before it reaches your inbox, Android requires KALKAN to be the default SMS app. While KALKAN holds that role it can read, write and send SMS/MMS. You may remove the role at any time from system settings; KALKAN then stops all such access immediately.

MMS note: KALKAN supports receiving and sending multimedia messages. Some carrier-specific behaviour has not been validated on every network.
