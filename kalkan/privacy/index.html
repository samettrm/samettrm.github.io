The short version

  • Message content and the links you check are processed on your device and are never sent to KALKAN. Sender and caller numbers are processed on your device too; only a number you block or report as spam is sent to our server without your identity, if community reports are on (the default, §2a).
  • Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we do not store your name or email address; we keep only a pseudonymous account ID.
  • To improve the app we measure usage with a pseudonymous installation ID: which screens are opened, which features are used. There is no advertising, no cross-app tracking and no sale of data.
  • The KALKAN Pro subscription is purchased through the App Store or Google Play; RevenueCat provides the subscription infrastructure.

2a. Community reports (on by default)

When you block a number in KALKAN (Lists, Activity, or Block in the call notification) or report it as spam with KALKAN from Recents on iPhone, that number and your vote are sent to our server with your pseudonymous installation ID. When you say “Not spam”, a counter-vote is sent. Message content, your contacts, your name and your email address are never sent.

  • Purpose: to block, for all KALKAN Pro users, a number that at least three different KALKAN Pro users with a verified purchase reported as spam in the last 90 days. If “Not spam” votes reach half of the spam votes, the number leaves the list.
  • Whose vote counts: only users whose KALKAN Pro comes from a real store purchase; we check your subscription status at RevenueCat for this. Reports from installations whose vote does not count are not stored.
  • Retention: votes are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. If you delete your account or your installation record, your votes are deleted too.
  • Turning it off: Settings › Community reports. While it is off, pending reports are not sent either.
  • A caller's number is a third person's data. We process it on the basis of our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and unwanted calls. Anyone who believes their number is on the list by mistake can object at bsdigitalapp@gmail.com; we will review it and remove it.

3. Account and sign-in (optional)

KALKAN's protection works without an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google in Settings:

  • Apple or Google gives us an identity token, which we verify on our server.
  • What we store: a pseudonymous account ID derived from a one-way hash of the user ID your provider assigns, the provider's name (Apple/Google) and the time the account was created. We do not store your name or email address. With Sign in with Apple we do not request your name or email. With Google, your email address is read only at sign-in for verification and is not stored.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we store the refresh token Apple issues, encrypted. Its only purpose is to revoke KALKAN's connection at Apple when you delete your account.
  • If you sign in, your KALKAN Pro subscription is linked to your account: instead of the installation ID, RevenueCat receives a random ID created for your account, and KALKAN Pro also works on your other devices signed in with the same account. If you sign out, this device goes back to its installation ID.
  • We use data received from Google only for sign-in; we do not use it for advertising, sell it or share it. Our use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Subscription and purchases

KALKAN Pro is a yearly auto-renewing subscription purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details stay with Apple or Google; we never see them. RevenueCat provides the subscription infrastructure: it receives the store's purchase record and KALKAN's pseudonymous installation ID (or, if you signed in, the random ID of your account, §3), tells the app whether your subscription is active, and forwards purchase events (product, price, currency, country, store, time) to our server. You manage and cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

4.1 Earlier purchases on Android

If you bought KALKAN on Google Play when it was a paid app, the app verifies this with Google Play Integrity: a token signed by Google is sent to our server, and our server asks Google whether this installation is licensed. We use only that result; if it is licensed, a lifetime KALKAN Pro entitlement is assigned to your pseudonymous ID at RevenueCat.

5. Other network requests and service providers

5.1 Threat intelligence download (Android)

On Android, KALKAN downloads a signed database of spam rules, a local model and known fraud numbers. This is a plain GET; it carries no query parameters, request body, cookies, device identifier or account identifier.

5.2 Service providers

  • Cloudflare — hosts our server. It technically processes your IP address while connecting; we do not store IP addresses.
  • RevenueCat — subscription infrastructure (§4).
  • Apple — App Store, Sign in with Apple, the in-app rating prompt.
  • Google — Google Play, Sign in with Google, Play Integrity, the in-app rating prompt.

These providers' servers may be located outside Türkiye (for example in the European Union and the United States). Data is transferred only for the purposes described in this policy.

6. Android default SMS app

On Android, KALKAN asks to become your default SMS app. The operating system requires this: only the default app can see an incoming message before it reaches your inbox, which is what makes real quarantine possible.

While it holds this role, KALKAN can read, write and send SMS/MMS on your device. If you take the role back, reading, writing and sending stop immediately — this is enforced in code on every privileged call.

As your default SMS app, KALKAN saves every incoming message, including spam it quarantines, to Android's system message storage. Quarantined messages are hidden in KALKAN's inbox and shown only on the Quarantine screen. That storage belongs to your phone, not to KALKAN, so:

  • Uninstalling KALKAN or switching to another SMS app does not delete these messages. Another messaging app may show them.
  • If your phone's own backup or device-transfer feature covers text messages (for example, backup to your Google account), quarantined messages may be included in that backup like your other texts. KALKAN does not manage those backups and they never reach KALKAN.
  • If a message cannot be written to the system storage the moment it arrives (for example, because KALKAN lost the default SMS role at that moment), KALKAN keeps it in its own storage and moves it to the system storage at the first opportunity. The Quarantine screen marks these messages. If you uninstall KALKAN before they are moved, they are deleted.
  • When KALKAN moves an earlier version's quarantine into the system storage, it keeps a copy of that old quarantine file in its own storage as a safety backup. The copy is never sent anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall KALKAN. If you delete one of those messages, its backup copy is deleted too.

You can permanently delete a quarantined message from the Quarantine screen; KALKAN asks for your confirmation first. Incoming message notifications are created on your device; quarantine notifications deliberately do not show the message body.

7. Retention and deletion

  • KALKAN's on-device data (your lists, activity counters, settings, the pseudonymous installation ID) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app; backup and device transfer are disabled for this data.
  • Community report votes are kept for 90 days and purged automatically every day (§2a).
  • Usage records on our server are kept for at most 24 months; the server purges them automatically every day. When you uninstall the app, the installation ID is deleted from your device and the server records can no longer be linked to you.
  • Delete account (Settings): your account, your sessions, the Apple refresh token (also revoked at Apple) the usage records of installations linked to your account and your account's record at RevenueCat are deleted. Purchase records remain, de-identified, for accounting. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

8. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs
  • No access to the advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID); no tracking across apps and websites
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs
  • No sale of personal data

9. Children

KALKAN is not directed at children under 13.

10. Your rights

Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to access your data, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. You can delete your account in the app under Settings › Delete account. For other requests: bsdigitalapp@gmail.com. We process data to provide the subscription and the account (performance of a contract) and to improve the product and keep it secure (legitimate interests).
