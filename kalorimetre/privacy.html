Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kalorimetre ("the app", "we") handles your information. We built Kalorimetre to be private by default: your data is stored on your device first, and only synced to the cloud to back it up and keep it across your devices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2.Data We Process

To provide nutrition tracking and AI features, the app processes:

  • Nutrition logs: meals, foods, portions, water, recipes and notes you add.
  • Body & activity metrics: weight, steps and goals you enter or that you allow us to read from Apple Health / Health Connect.
  • Meal photos: when you use AI photo analysis, the image is sent to our AI provider to estimate calories and macros. Photos are processed to generate results and are not used to identify you.
  • Voice input: if you use voice logging, your audio is transcribed to text by our AI provider (Google Gemini) through our secure server.
  • AI coach messages: the questions you send to the in-app coach ("Betty") and the related nutrition context.
  • Account info: an anonymous account is created automatically; optionally you may link an email, Apple or Google account to back up your data.

3.How We Use Your Data

  • To calculate your calories, macros, micronutrients and progress.
  • To generate AI estimates, insights and personalized coaching.
  • To back up and sync your data across your devices.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

4.Health Data

With your explicit permission, the app may read activity data (steps, active energy, weight) from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), and may write the meals and water you log back to them. Health data is used only inside the app for your tracking and is never sold or used for advertising. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

5.In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, and managed via RevenueCat. We never receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat receives a randomized app user ID and purchase status to keep your subscription active across devices.

6.Advertising

The free version may display ads through Google AdMob. On iOS, before any ad tracking, you will be asked for permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Premium removes ads entirely.

7.Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — secure backend, authentication and cloud sync.
  • Google Gemini — AI analysis of meal photos, text and voice transcription (via our secure server).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing.
  • Google AdMob — advertising in the free version.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

8.Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays until you delete it. You can reset all local data from the app's Settings, and unlink your account at any time. To request deletion of cloud-synced data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

9.Children's Privacy

Kalorimetre is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10.Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
